# Privacy Policy — Fortune Bay Support Bot

**Effective date:** 12 October 2026  
**Last updated:** 12 October 2026

Fortune Bay operates the Telegram support bot **@FortuneBay_SupportBot** (the “Bot”). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Bot and how that information may be used.

## 1. Contact

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete your information, contact **@FortuneBay_SupportBot** on Telegram.

## 2. Information We Process

When you use the Bot, the following information may be processed, depending on how the support service operates:

- Your Telegram user ID, display name, and username.
- Messages, images, documents, and other files you choose to send.
- Information you voluntarily provide when requesting customer support.
- Support conversation identifiers, timestamps, and message delivery information needed to manage support requests.
- Referral or source information, if you start the Bot through a tracked link.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, account credentials, or other sensitive information unless it is specifically required through an appropriate and secure process.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

- Create and manage customer-support conversations.
- Identify and respond to your support requests.
- Provide assistance related to Fortune Bay services, games, bonuses, or other available offerings.
- Maintain service security and help prevent spam, fraud, and misuse.
- Troubleshoot technical issues and improve the support experience.
- Meet applicable legal obligations.

## 4. Who May Receive Information

Your support messages and relevant Telegram information may be accessible to authorized Fortune Bay support personnel who need the information to assist you.

The Bot uses Telegram for messaging. If hosting, database, or other technical service providers are used, those providers may process relevant information as necessary to operate the service.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, rights, or service security.

**Fortune Bay does not sell personal information.**

## 5. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle support requests, maintain service security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal or record-keeping obligations.

The actual retention period depends on Fortune Bay's data-storage and deletion practices. Information may be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Telegram may retain chat information according to its own settings, policies, and applicable obligations.

## 6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information and restrict access to authorized personnel. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Please keep your Telegram account secure and avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information in support conversations.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to exercise other privacy rights.

To submit a request, contact **@FortuneBay_SupportBot** on Telegram. Include your Telegram username or user ID, where possible, so the relevant support record can be identified. Additional verification may be required.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or record-keeping purposes.

## 8. International Processing

Telegram and any technical service providers used to operate the Bot may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be applied to international transfers.

## 9. Children

The Bot is not intended for individuals who do not meet the minimum age requirements applicable to Telegram or the services offered in their location.

If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact **@FortuneBay_SupportBot** so the matter can be reviewed.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Fortune Bay's services, data-handling practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version should be published at the policy URL, along with the updated revision date.
